Why standardise first

Salt forms of one drug must merge into one compound, and scaffolds must exist before splitting or the drug-cold split would leak structural analogues across train/test.

Why 4 different splits

Each asks a harder question: interpolate (random) → unseen chemistry (drug-cold) → unseen protein (target-cold, the real test) → future data (temporal).

Why a leakage check in code

If one scaffold or protein landed on both sides, every later accuracy number would be inflated and nothing downstream would reveal it. The build fails rather than ship a leak.
