The four train / test splits, and the question each one answers

Every split also carves a small calibration slice from its own training data, kept for the Week 7 uncertainty step. A leakage check confirms nothing held out for testing also appears in training.
SplitWhat is held out for testingQuestion it answersWhy it matters
RandomA random 15% of pairsCan the model interpolate within chemistry and biology it has already seen?The easiest test — a sanity floor. Good scores here alone prove little.
Drug-coldWhole scaffolds (core ring systems)Does it work on chemical structures it has never seen?Grouping by scaffold stops near-identical molecules leaking across the split.
Target-cold primaryWhole proteinsDoes it work on a protein it has never seen in training?This is what real drug repurposing actually is — so it is the headline test.
TemporalEverything measured in 2020 or laterDoes it work on data from the future, trained only on the past?The closest thing to a genuine prospective test. Limited by the 40% of records with no date.
Fixed random seed 42, recorded in the manifest, so the exact same splits regenerate on any machine. Rules: DECISIONS.md D-019.
