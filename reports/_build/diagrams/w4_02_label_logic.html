Which measurements count (D-011)

USED Affinity dose-response: IC50, Ki, Kd, EC50 (2.77M rows)
INACTIVE only qHTS screens (Potency, AC50) at ≥10µM no-activity
EXCLUDED Kinetics (kon/koff, 1.37M) — not an affinity
EXCLUDED %-inhibition / single-point (1.02M) — too crude

Restricting to dose-response affinities nearly removes the PubChem distortion found in Week 1 (median pChEMBL 6.85 → 6.91 without PubChem).

Also INACTIVE without an exact value

A pair with no exact affinity is still INACTIVE if it has any of:

  • a censored affinity > 10µM (assay stopped, no activity)
  • a "not active" / "no activity" lab comment
  • a qHTS screen at ≥10µM with no activity

This is why censored rows were protected in Week 1 — they are most of our negative evidence.

UNTESTED (no usable measurement, or "inconclusive") is never turned into INACTIVE and is not put in the table.
